# STAGE 2.5 — MARKETING CLAIMS AND CONTENT CORRECTIONS

## Objective
Correct unsupported, misleading, fictional or inaccurate public-facing claims.

## Audit areas
- Homepage statistics and testimonials.
- Pricing and plan features.
- Product and integration claims.
- Uptime and SLA statements.
- Security and compliance claims.
- Open-source and self-hosting claims.
- Voice, CLI, desktop application and status-page claims.
- Markcopilot AI capabilities.
- M-Pesa and fraud-prevention claims.
- Contact information, addresses and telephone numbers.
- Legal and privacy statements.

## Requirements
- Verify every material claim against reliable implementation evidence.
- Distinguish implemented features from documentation, plans and assumptions.
- Remove or correct unsupported claims.
- Do not invent replacement statistics, testimonials, contact details or promises.
- Do not make new claims merely to fill empty sections.
- Preserve useful accurate content.
- Keep existing brand assets.

Do not describe Chatwoot Community Edition capabilities as MarkChat capabilities unless the integration and implementation are verified.

## Approval gate
Present a before-and-after claim register and the corrected public content for owner review. Stop before Stage 2.6.
