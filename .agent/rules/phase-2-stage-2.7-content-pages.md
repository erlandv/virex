# STAGE 2.7 — PRODUCT, SOLUTIONS AND RESOURCE PAGES

## Objective
Improve product, feature, solution and resource pages for accuracy, completeness and meaningful navigation.

## Requirements
- Audit existing routes and dynamic content before editing.
- Preserve useful existing content.
- Verify each product capability before presenting it as implemented.
- Distinguish product features, integrations, use cases and planned functionality.
- Ensure solution pages contain relevant and accurate workflows rather than repeated generic copy.
- Ensure resource pages and documentation links resolve correctly.
- Remove or correct phantom routes and unsupported offerings.
- Ensure internal links, breadcrumbs and calls to action are valid.
- Preserve existing CMS scope; these pages are not additional CMS modules.
- Avoid duplicating content simply to increase page count.

## Approval gate
Provide a route inventory, content review and list of unresolved claims. Stop for owner approval before Stage 2.8.
