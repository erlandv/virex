Over 94% of smartphone owners in Kenya use WhatsApp daily. Most small and medium businesses start by buying a dedicated smartphone with WhatsApp Business App installed. But as order volume scales, this approach creates serious operational bottlenecks:

## The Hazards of Physical Device Commerce
- **Single Agent Bottleneck:** Only one person can hold the phone. During sales rushes (e.g. Black Friday or month-end paydays), customers wait hours for a reply.
- **Lost Chat History:** If the phone is lost, stolen, or damaged, years of customer order history disappear.
- **Devastating Number Bans:** Sending bulk order updates or promos from consumer WhatsApp frequently triggers Meta spam detection, permanently banning your business number.

## The Meta Cloud API Advantage
MarkChat connects your official phone number directly to Meta Cloud API:
- Up to 50 sales agents can reply simultaneously on computers and phones.
- Role-based permissions keep sensitive customer data safe.
- Official pre-approved message templates guarantee deliverability without ban risks.
- In-chat `/stk` shortcuts allow agents to close sales right inside the conversation thread.
