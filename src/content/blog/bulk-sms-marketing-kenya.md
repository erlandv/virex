While messaging apps dominate peer-to-peer conversations, Bulk SMS remains the gold standard for transactional delivery in Kenya. With over 98% open rates within 3 minutes, transactional SMS reaches every Safaricom and Airtel subscriber, even when mobile data is turned off.

## Two Flexible Ways to Run Bulk SMS on MarkChat

### 1. Managed Prepaid Bundles
Ideal for growing merchants who want instant setup without negotiating telco carrier contracts.
- Start with 50 free trial SMS credits on signup.
- Top up anytime via instant M-Pesa STK push.
- Benefit from MarkChat’s pre-warmed carrier routes and automatic delivery retry logic.

### 2. Bring Your Own Carrier (BYOC)
Ideal for established retail chains, clinics, and enterprises with custom negotiated rates:
- Plug in your own Africa’s Talking API Key and Username.
- Use your legally registered alphanumeric Sender ID (e.g. `YOURBRAND`).
- Enjoy 0% platform markup on all SMS messages sent.
