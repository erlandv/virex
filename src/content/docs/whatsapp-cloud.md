Connecting to the official Meta Cloud API allows multiple agents to manage customer conversations simultaneously on a single Kenyan phone number.

## Prerequisites
- A verified Facebook Business Manager account
- A dedicated phone number capable of receiving an SMS or voice verification code (cannot be currently registered on the consumer WhatsApp app)

## Embedded 1-Click Signup
1. Navigate to **MarkChat > Channels > WhatsApp**.
2. Click **Connect with Facebook**.
3. Select your Business Manager profile and choose your WhatsApp Business account.
4. Input your Kenyan phone number (+254...) and verify via 6-digit SMS code.
5. Once verified, incoming messages route immediately into your MarkChat team inbox.

## Key Features
- **Shared Inbox:** Assign inquiries to individual agents or departments.
- **Internal Notes:** Leave private comments on customer threads visible only to team members.
- **Canned Responses:** Send instant answers to common delivery or pricing questions.
