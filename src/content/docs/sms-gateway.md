MarkChat is connected to Africa’s Talking and Kenyan telco aggregators to ensure sub-second transactional message delivery.

## Option A: Managed Prepaid Bundles
1. Every newly registered MarkChat workspace includes **50 free trial SMS credits**.
2. Top up credits anytime directly in the workspace using in-app M-Pesa STK push.
3. Messages route through MarkChat’s high-priority shared alphanumeric sender IDs.

## Option B: Bring Your Own Carrier (BYOC)
For organizations with high monthly volume or existing telco sender IDs:
1. Navigate to **Settings > Integrations > Bulk SMS**.
2. Switch mode to **Bring Your Own Carrier (BYOC)**.
3. Input your **Africa's Talking Username**, **API Key**, and your approved **Alphanumeric Sender ID**.
4. MarkChat delivers messages directly through your carrier credentials with **KES 0 platform markup**.
