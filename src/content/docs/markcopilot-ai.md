Markcopilot is a dual-mode AI engine fine-tuned for Kenyan conversational commerce.

## 1. Uploading Documents to the Knowledge Vault
To ensure Markcopilot answers questions accurately:
1. Navigate to **Markcopilot > Knowledge Vault**.
2. Upload your store price lists (PDF), restaurant menus, clinic consultation fee schedules, or return policies.
3. Markcopilot indexes item names, variants, pricing, and delivery zones.

## 2. Customer-Facing 24/7 Mode
When enabled:
- Markcopilot greets customers automatically in English, Conversational Swahili, or Sheng.
- Answers questions about stock availability and delivery fees (e.g. CBD KES 150, Westlands KES 250).
- Prepares `/stk` checkout prompts when customers confirm purchase intent.

## 3. Agent Copilot Mode
When operating alongside human agents:
- Sits in the inbox and generates 1-click reply suggestions for every incoming inquiry.
- Summarizes multi-day conversation histories in 3 bullet points.
- Flags urgent orders or dissatisfied customer sentiment for supervisor escalation.
